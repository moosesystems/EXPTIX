❓ What is ExpTix, and why does it exist?

ExpTix is the hotel's shared job list. Anyone can tell it "this needs doing". It sends the job to the right team, starts a timer, reminds people, and remembers everything that happened.

📘 What it is

One website, on any phone, tablet or computer, where every department logs and tracks its work: repairs, guest requests, complaints, room cleaning, food safety checks, events, incidents and shift notes. Think of it as the hotel's to-do list, fix-it list and logbook, all in one place that everyone can see.

💚 Why it exists

A hotel runs on hundreds of small jobs a day, passed between people and shifts. Without one shared place, jobs live in radio calls, phone messages, sticky notes and people's heads. Some get forgotten, the guest waits, and nobody can tell what happened. ExpTix makes sure every job has an owner, a deadline and a record.

👉 How it works

Someone reports a job. ExpTix reads it, picks the team and the timer, and alerts the people on shift. They tap Start and Done. If time runs out, a supervisor hears about it. Afterwards, managers see the numbers.

Before and after

😟 Without a shared system
  • A guest asks the front desk for towels. The message goes by radio, and maybe arrives.
  • Night shift sees a leak. Day shift never hears about it.
  • The same toilet breaks five times and nobody notices the pattern.
  • Fridge temperatures are written on paper that is hard to find during an inspection.
  • The GM asks "how fast do we fix things?" and nobody knows.
😊 With ExpTix
  • The towel request goes straight to housekeeping's phones, with a timer. If the guest asked by QR code, they can watch it on their own phone.
  • The leak is a ticket with photos. Day shift sees it on their screen and in the logbook.
  • ExpTix flags the room as a repeat problem so engineering fixes the cause.
  • Temperatures are logged in seconds. A bad reading opens an urgent job by itself.
  • Reports show response times, guest ratings and problem rooms for any week or month.

✅ What ExpTix is

  • The work system for every department: front office, housekeeping, engineering, kitchen, F&B, banquets, security, laundry and IT.
  • A way for guests to ask for things by scanning a QR code, with no app or sign-in.
  • A memory: who did what, when, and how long it took.

🚫 What ExpTix is not

  • Not Opera. Reservations, check-in, folios and billing stay in Opera Cloud. ExpTix listens to Opera and only shares room status and minibar charges back.
  • Not Canary. Guest messaging stays in Canary. Guest requests from Canary become ExpTix jobs.
  • Not an emergency system. In a real emergency, call 911 and use the radio first.

🔑 Getting started

📘 What

ExpTix is a website that acts like an app. Nothing to buy or download. It works on hotel computers, tablets and your own phone.

👉 How

Sign in once, put it on your phone's home screen, and turn on push alerts. Ten minutes, start to finish.

💚 Why

The phone in your pocket becomes your radio, notepad and job list. You can report a problem the moment you see it.

Sign in

  1. Open the ExpTix address your administrator gave you on any phone, tablet or computer.
  2. Type your username and password. Your manager gives you these.
  3. First time? ExpTix asks you to pick a new password. Use 12 or more characters. A short sentence works great: "Blue canoe on the lake".
  4. If you turned on two-step sign-in, type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

🙅 Never

Never share your password or sign in as someone else. ExpTix writes your name on everything you do.

Put it on your phone like an app 📱

  1. iPhone: open it in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open ExpTix from the new icon. Go to My account and tap Enable push alerts.
  4. Tap Send a test. Your phone should buzz. 🎉

💚 Why

With push alerts, new jobs find you. You do not have to keep checking.

🙋 Things everyone does

Whatever your job, these four skills cover most of your day.

➕ Report something (make a request)

📘 What

A request (we also call it a ticket) is a little card that says "this needs doing". It has a number like EXP-000123.

👉 How

  1. Tap the gold + New.
  2. Tap a Quick pick button, or just type what is wrong.
  3. Add the room number if there is one.
  4. Snap a photo if it helps.
  5. Tap Create request.

💚 Why

You do not need to know who fixes it. Leave Department blank and ExpTix reads your words and sends it to the right team. "Toilet running in 512" goes to Engineering all by itself.

💡 Tip

Is the room unusable until it is fixed? Tick Take the room out of order. The front desk will not sell it, and it comes back automatically when the job is resolved.

▶️ Work a job

📘 What

Every job moves along a simple path: New, then Acknowledged, then In progress, then Resolved.

👉 How

  1. Open the job (tap it).
  2. Not yours yet? Tap 🙋 Take it.
  3. Tap ▶ Start when you begin.
  4. Tick any checklist steps.
  5. Tap ✔ Resolve and write what you did.

💚 Why

The buttons stop the timers, tell the person who asked, and (for guests) tell the guest. One tap saves three phone calls.

New→Acknowledged→In progress→Resolved→Closed

💡 Stuck?

Waiting for a part or the guest is in the room? Tap ⏸ On hold and say why. Need another team too? Tap ➕ Follow-up for another team. It makes a linked job for them.

💬 Write an update

📘 What

The Activity box at the bottom of every job. It is the job's diary.

👉 How

Type your note, keep Internal note selected, tap Post. To talk to the guest, pick Message guest first (only shows when the guest asked through the QR code or Canary).

💚 Why

The next shift reads it and knows exactly where things stand. Internal notes are never shown to guests.

📓 Read and write the shift logbook

📘 What

The Shift logbook is the notebook that gets passed between shifts. "Room 610 asked for a quiet room tomorrow." "Ice machine on 4 is making noise."

👉 How

  1. Menu, then Shift logbook.
  2. Type your note, pick how important it is, tap Add to log.
  3. Starting a shift? Read the notes and tap Mark read.

💚 Why

Managers can see who read what. "Nobody told me" goes away.

🟢 Go on shift and off shift

📘 What

A switch on My work that says "I am here and can take jobs".

👉 How

Tick On shift when you start. Untick it when you go home.

💚 Why

ExpTix only hands new jobs to people who are on shift. Off shift means your phone stays quiet. 😴

Room attendant

Housekeeping staff and housepersons

🌅 Start of shift

  1. Open ExpTix and tick On shift on My work.
  2. Tap 🛏️ My rooms. You see only the rooms your supervisor gave you.
  3. Do gold-bordered rooms first. Those are guests arriving today. ⚡ means Rush: do it now.

🧹 Clean a room

📘 What

Each room is a coloured square. The colour says how clean it is. Red is dirty, orange is being cleaned, blue is clean, green is inspected.

👉 How

  1. Tap the room square.
  2. Tap ▶ Start cleaning. It turns orange.
  3. Clean the room. 🧽
  4. Tap ✔ Clean. It turns blue.

💚 Why

The front desk is watching. When your room turns blue, your supervisor inspects it, and then the guest can check in. Your taps get guests into rooms faster.

🚫 Guest says "do not disturb" or "no service today"

📘 What

DND means do not knock. Declined service means the guest said no cleaning today (they may get an F&B credit for it 🌿).

👉 How

Tap the room, tick DND or Declined service. Untick when it changes.

💚 Why

Your supervisor will not mark you late for a room you were not allowed into.

🔧 Something is broken in the room

📘 What

A dripping tap, a dead lamp, a stain on the carpet. Anything Engineering or another team should fix.

👉 How

  1. Tap the room square.
  2. Tap 🔧 Report a problem. The room number is already filled in.
  3. Type what is wrong, add a photo, tap Create request.

💚 Why

It goes straight to the right person. The 🔧 icon shows on the room until it is fixed so nobody forgets.

🧳 Found something a guest left

👉 How

Menu, Lost and found, + New. Say what it is, where you found it and the room. Hand it to your supervisor.

💚 Why

When the guest calls, the front desk finds it in seconds.

💡 Little icons on a room

⭐ VIP guest · 🚫 do not disturb · 🧹 guest asked for service · ⚡ rush · 🌿 declined service · 🔧 open repair

Engineering

Maintenance techs and engineers

🎫 Work orders

📘 What

A work order is a "please fix this" job. It may say a room, a place (like the Boiler room) or a machine (an asset, like "Dryer 1").

👉 How

  1. Open My work. Red stripes are most urgent.
  2. ▶ Start, fix it, then log your minutes and any parts under Log time and parts.
  3. ✔ Resolve and write what you did.
  4. Was the room out of order? Leave Return room to service ticked.

💚 Why

Parts come off the stock count by themselves. Time and parts add up on the machine's history, so the chief can see which machine to replace.

💡 Look before you fix

The right side of a job shows Room history and Asset history. 🔁 means this keeps happening. Maybe the real problem is somewhere else.

📆 Preventive maintenance (PM)

📘 What

Jobs that come back on a schedule: clean the dryer vents every month, test the fire pump every week. ExpTix makes these jobs by itself.

👉 How

They appear in your list like any job, with a checklist. Do each step, tick it, resolve.

💚 Why

Fixing before it breaks is cheaper and guests never notice. Inspectors also want proof PM was done; this is the proof.

📦 Parts and purchase requests

👉 How

Parts and stock shows what is on the shelf. Need to buy something? Purchase requests, + New. Your manager approves it. Tap 📥 Received when it arrives.

💚 Why

No more "I thought someone ordered it".

Kitchen

Chefs, cooks and stewards

🌡️ Temperature checks (food safety)

📘 What

Every fridge, freezer and hot-holding unit has a tile on the Food safety page. Orange border means a check is due. Red means the last reading was unsafe.

👉 How

  1. Menu, Food safety.
  2. Tap the unit.
  3. Type the temperature, tap Save reading.
  4. If it is out of range, write what you did (moved the food, called Engineering).

💚 Why

An unsafe reading opens an urgent job for the kitchen and Engineering by itself. And the health inspector gets a clean, signed log instead of a wet clipboard. Some units have sensors 📡 that log by themselves.

🚫 The 86 list

📘 What

"86" means we ran out. The 86 list shows what is unavailable and what to offer instead.

👉 How

Menu, 86 list, + New: the item, the outlet, a substitute. Turn it off when it is back.

💚 Why

Servers see it on Home, and guests see "not available today" on their QR page. No more disappointed orders.

🥜 Allergy alerts

👉 How

Menu, Allergy alerts. Check before the event or the room service order. Add new ones with + New.

💚 Why

Allergies are the one thing we can never get wrong. Everyone sees the same list.

💡 Broken equipment?

+ New, quick pick Kitchen equipment. It goes straight to Engineering as a high priority.

Food and beverage

Servers, lounge, in-room dining

Your jobs are guest requests for your outlet, in-room dining trays to pick up, and anything broken in your area.

👉 How

  1. Check Home for the 86 list and events today.
  2. Check Allergy alerts and Guest preferences for VIPs.
  3. A guest complains at the table? + New, type Guest complaint, write what happened and what you did.

💚 Why

💡 Tray left in the hallway?

Anyone can log it. Quick pick Tray pickup or just type "tray outside 412".

Banquets and events

Event coordinators and banquet staff

📘 What

An event (or BEO, banquet event order) is the plan for a meeting, wedding or conference: the room, the time, the head count, the menu and the setup.

👉 How

  1. Menu, Events and BEOs, + New.
  2. Fill in the room, times, guests, setup, menu, AV and any allergies.
  3. Save. ExpTix makes a task for each team that needs to help (setup, kitchen, AV, housekeeping).

💚 Why

One form tells every department what to do and by when. Each team ticks off its own task, so you can see who is ready.

💡 See what is coming

Menu, Calendar shows the next month of events, maintenance and scheduled work together.

Security

Officers and leads

📝 Incident reports

📘 What

A record of anything that went wrong: a fall, a fight, a theft, damage, a fire alarm, a medical call.

👉 How

  1. Make sure everyone is safe first. 🚑
  2. Menu, Incident reports, + New.
  3. Write what happened, when, where, who was there and what you did. Attach photos.

💚 Why

Insurance, the police and the workers' compensation board may ask for it. A clear report written right away protects the guest, the staff and the hotel. Serious ones alert the managers right away.

🔑 Key control

👉 How

Menu, Key control. Log every master key or radio that goes out: who, when, and when it came back.

💚 Why

A missing master key is a big deal. This shows who had it last.

Laundry

Laundry attendants

📘 What

Your jobs: linen requests from floors, machine problems, and the dryer vent cleaning that comes up on a schedule.

👉 How

Work them from My work like any job. A machine acting up? + New, pick the machine under Asset, describe it.

💚 Why

Front desk

Guest services, PBX, night audit

☎️ A guest calls or walks up with a request

📘 What

You are the hotel's ears. You can see every department's jobs so you can answer "where is my towel?"

👉 How

  1. + New. Tap a quick pick (Extra towels or linen, Room too cold, Noise complaint).
  2. Type the room. Add the guest's name.
  3. Set Source to Phone call or Front desk. Tick VIP if they are.
  4. Create request. Tell the guest the number.

💚 Why

The guest request clock starts right away. If it is late, managers get told. When it is done, you can call the guest back with confidence.

🛏️ Rooms for check-in

👉 How

  1. Open Rooms. Filter Any reservation to Due in.
  2. Green rooms are ready. Need one fast? Tap it, tick Rush.
  3. A room cannot be sold? Tap it, type a reason, Set OOO (out of order).

💚 Why

Housekeeping sees your rush right away, without a phone call. Out-of-order rooms go back to Opera so nobody sells them by mistake.

😠 A complaint

👉 How

Log it as a Guest complaint. In the job, fill in Resolution and service recovery: what you did (free breakfast, room move) and what it cost.

💚 Why

Managers see every complaint and every dollar given back, and can spot patterns. A guest who rates us 1 or 2 stars alerts the front office for a callback.

💡 Also yours

🍫 Minibar: tap an occupied room, 🍫 Minibar, post the charge (it goes to the Opera folio). ⭐ Guest preferences: feather-free pillows, high floor, allergies. Add them and every team sees them on that guest's jobs.

Supervisor

Shift leads, housekeeping and engineering supervisors

You can do everything your team does, plus these.

👥 Hand out the work

📘 What

Every room has credits (how much work it is). A suite is more than a standard room. Each attendant can do about 14 to 16 credits a shift.

👉 How

  1. Rooms, tap ⚖ Auto-assign. ExpTix shares the rooms fairly between attendants on shift, keeping floors together.
  2. Want to change it? ☑ Select rooms, tap some rooms, pick a name, Assign.
  3. For other jobs, use the assign drop-down on the job.

💚 Why

Fair, fast and visible. The bars on the right show who is full and who can take more.

✅ Inspect

📘 What

A checklist you walk through: a room inspection, an opening check, a fire safety walk.

👉 How

  1. Inspections (or tap a room, 📋 Inspect).
  2. Pick the checklist. For each line tap ✓, ✕ or N/A.
  3. A ✕ asks "what is wrong?". Type a few words.
  4. Submit. You get a score.
  5. For a passed room, tap the room and press ★ Inspected. It turns green.

💚 Why

Every ✕ becomes a job for the right team automatically. Scores over time show who needs coaching and who deserves a thank-you. 🏆

📊 Watch the clock

👉 How

Home shows Overdue, SLA breached and Unassigned counts. Tap a number to see those jobs. Check Reports at the end of the week.

💚 Why

You catch a late job before the guest calls about it.

Department manager

Executive housekeeper, chief engineer, F&B manager, chef, front office manager

You can do everything a supervisor does, plus set up how your department works.

📘 What you control

  • Request categories: the quick-pick buttons and the words that route jobs to you.
  • SLA policies: how fast each kind of job must be answered and finished.
  • Checklist templates: your inspections and procedures.
  • Preventive maintenance, Assets, Vendors and Notification rules.
  • Purchase requests: approve or reject.

👉 How

Open the item from the menu, tap a row to change it, or + New. A checklist template is one step per line. Start a line with ## to make a heading.

💚 Why

You set the rules once. ExpTix follows them every time, on every shift, at 3 a.m. too.

💡 Vendor paperwork

Put insurance and WCB expiry dates on each vendor. ExpTix warns you before they run out.

General manager

The whole property

You see every department, every room, every report. You also manage Users, Integrations, and API and MCP.

👉 How

Real numbers instead of guesses. "Room 317 has had 6 plumbing calls this month" tells you where to spend money.

Administrator

System owner (hotel IT or your IT provider)

👤 Add a person

👉 How

  1. Menu, Users, + New.
  2. Name, username, role, department (and extra departments if they float).
  3. Create. Copy the temporary password and give it to them in person. It is shown only once.

💚 Why

The role decides what they can see and do (see Who sees what). They must pick their own password on first sign-in.

💡 Someone leaves or forgets

Open the user: 🔑 Reset password, 🔓 Unlock (after 5 wrong tries), ↺ Reset MFA (lost phone). To remove access, untick Active. Do not delete people, so their history stays.

🔗 Integrations (Opera, Canary and friends)

📘 What

Links to other systems. Opera Cloud sends room and guest status in and gets housekeeping status and minibar charges back. Canary turns guest text messages into jobs. Also: email, SMS, Teams, sensors.

👉 How

Integrations, fill in the details from the vendor, switch Mock to Live, Save. Mock pretends to be the other system so you can demo safely.

💚 Why

Nobody types the same thing twice. Passwords for these links are locked away (encrypted) and never shown again.

🤖 API and MCP (computers and AI assistants)

📘 What

A token is a key that lets another program, or an AI assistant like Claude, use ExpTix. It acts as the person you choose, with that person's access.

👉 How

API and MCP, name the token, pick who it acts as, tick scopes (read, write, mcp, admin), set an expiry, Create token. Copy it now; it is shown once. Full details are in the API documentation.

💚 Why

An AI assistant can give the morning briefing, file guest requests from messages, or answer "what is broken on floor 6?". Everything it does is logged under its token.

🔐 Audit log and security

📘 What

The Audit log records every sign-in and every change. Each line is chained to the one before, like a wax seal, so changes cannot be hidden. Security and compliance shows how ExpTix lines up with NIST security controls.

👉 How

Audit log, Verify integrity. A ✓ means nothing was tampered with. Filter by person or action.

💚 Why

Auditors, insurers and owners can trust the record.

🔳 Print QR codes for guests

👉 How

Room QR codes, pick a floor, 🖨 Print. Put one on each desk card or mirror, plus the lobby, pool and gym.

💚 Why

Each code knows its room. Guests scan, tap, done. No app, no sign-in, no phone tag.

🎨 Types and colours

Every coloured tag in ExpTix means something. Here is the picture dictionary.

📘 What

Each job has a type (what kind of job), a status (where it is), a priority (how urgent) and a source (where it came from). Each one has its own colour.

👉 How

When you tap + New, pick the type that matches best. Not sure? Pick Work order for anything broken and Guest request for anything a guest asked for. A supervisor can change it later.

💚 Why

The type sets the timer and the team. The right type gets the right people moving at the right speed.

⏱️ The timers (SLA)

📘 What

Every job gets two invisible kitchen timers. Respond by: someone must say "got it". Resolve by: it must be done. Guest requests have short timers. Painting has long ones.

👉 How

Watch the little label on each job: Due in 2 h, Due in 20 min, Overdue 1 h. Tap 👍 Acknowledge or ▶ Start to stop the first timer.

💚 Why

No job sits forgotten. If time runs out, ExpTix tells a supervisor, then a manager. That is a safety net, not a punishment.

🆘 Uh oh! Quick fixes

📘 What

The six snags people hit most, and the fix for each.

👉 How

Find the sentence that sounds like you and follow it. Still stuck? Ask your supervisor, then an admin.

💚 Why

Most fixes take one minute. You do not need to wait for IT.

😕 "I cannot sign in."

Check caps lock. After 5 wrong tries, wait 15 minutes or ask a manager to Unlock you. Lost your phone with the code app? Ask an admin to Reset MFA.

🙈 "I cannot see a job."

You may only see your own department. Ask your supervisor, or ask the person to assign it to you. Also check the Open / All / Done switch on Tickets.

🔕 "My phone does not buzz."

Tick On shift. On iPhone, ExpTix must be opened from the Home Screen icon. Then My account, Enable push alerts, Send a test.

✋ "The Resolve button warns me."

Some checklist steps are not ticked. Tick them, or resolve anyway and say why in the note.

🔁 "I made a mistake."

Change the field and save, or tap ✕ Cancel with a reason. A closed job can be reopened with ▶ Resume / reopen. Everything is kept in the history, so nothing is lost.

📵 "It says I am offline."

Check Wi-Fi. ExpTix needs a connection to save your work. Write it down and log it as soon as you are back.

🚨 Real emergency?

Fire, medical, or someone in danger: call 911 and follow the hotel emergency plan first. Log it in ExpTix afterwards.
