Room attendant

Your rooms, your phone, four taps

One-page sheetFor: Room attendants

Start your shift

  1. 🟢 Open My work. Tick On shift.
  2. 🛏️ Tap My rooms. These are yours.
  3. 🟨 Gold border? A guest comes today. Clean it first.
  4. ⚡ Rush? Clean it now.

Room colours

Colour It means
🟥 Red Dirty
🟧 Orange Cleaning now
🟦 Blue Clean
🟩 Green Checked. Ready for a guest.

Little icons: ⭐ VIP guest. 🚫 Do not disturb. 🧹 Guest wants cleaning. 🔧 Something to fix.

Clean a room

  1. 👆 Tap the room.
  2. ▶️ Tap Start cleaning. The room turns orange.
  3. 🧽 Clean the room.
  4. ✔️ Tap Clean. The room turns blue.

The guest says no

  • 🚫 Do not knock? Tick DND.
  • 🌿 No cleaning today? Tick Declined service.

Something is broken

  1. 👆 Tap the room.
  2. 🔧 Tap Report a problem.
  3. ✍️ Say what is wrong. Add a photo.
  4. 📨 Tap Create request.

A guest left something

🧳 Tap ☰, Lost and found, + New. Say what and where. Give it to your supervisor.

If something is wrong

  • 😕 Cannot sign in? Ask your supervisor.
  • 🔕 Phone is quiet? Tick On shift.
  • 🚨 Fire or danger? Call 911 first.

Why this helps you: your taps show your good work. Nobody marks you late for a room you could not enter.
