Maintenance tech

Fix it, log it, done

One-page sheetFor: Maintenance techs and engineers
⬇ PDF to print⬇ Word⬇ Markdown

Start your shift

  1. 🟢 Open My work. Tick On shift.
  2. 🔴 Red stripe? Most urgent. Do it first.

Fix a job

  1. 👆 Tap the job. Not yours? Tap Take it.
  2. 🔁 Look at Asset history and the room history on the job. 🔁 means it keeps breaking. Look for the real cause.
  3. ▶️ Tap Start.
  4. 🛠️ Fix it.
  5. ⏱️ Under Log time and parts, type your minutes. Tap Log. Pick each part. Tap Use.
  6. ✔️ Tap Resolve. Write one line: “Replaced flapper.”
  7. 🛏️ Room was out of order? Leave Return room to service ticked.

PM jobs

📆 PM jobs come by themselves on a schedule. Example: clean dryer vents. Do each checklist step. Tick it. Tap Resolve.

Waiting or need help

  • ⏸️ Waiting for a part? Tap On hold. Say why.
  • ➕ Need another team? Tap Follow-up for another team.

If something is wrong

  • ✋ Resolve warns you? Tick the checklist steps, or say why.
  • 🙈 Cannot see a job? Ask your supervisor.
  • 🚨 Fire or danger? Call 911 first.

Why this helps you: your time and parts build the machine’s history, so the chief can replace bad machines instead of you fixing them again.
