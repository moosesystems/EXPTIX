Front desk agent

Every guest request, one place

One-page sheetFor: Front desk, guest services and night audit
⬇ PDF to print⬇ Word⬇ Markdown

A guest calls or walks up

  1. ➕ Tap + New.
  2. 👆 Tap a quick pick. Example: Extra towels or linen.
  3. 🚪 Type the room. Type the guest’s name.
  4. ☎️ Set Source to Phone call or Front desk.
  5. ⭐ VIP? Tick VIP guest.
  6. 📨 Tap Create request. Tell the guest the number.

Guest tracking link

🔗 Open the guest request. Next to Guest tracking link, tap Copy. Send it to the guest.

Rooms for check-in

  1. 🛏️ Open Rooms. Set Any reservation to Due in.
  2. 🟩 Green rooms are ready.
  3. ⚡ Need one fast? Tap it. Tick Rush.

Out of order

🚧 Tap the room. Type a reason. Tap Set OOO. Fixed? Tap Return to service.

A complaint

  1. 😠 Log it as a Guest complaint.
  2. 🎁 In the job, fill in Resolution and service recovery: what you gave and what it cost.

Minibar

🍫 Tap an occupied room. Tap Minibar. Post the charge.

If something is wrong

  • ⭐ Guest gave 1 or 2 stars? Call them back.
  • 😕 Stuck? Ask your supervisor.

Why this helps you: you can answer “where is my towel?” in seconds, with no phone calls.
