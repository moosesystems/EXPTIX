Owners and auditors

See the real records, change nothing

One-page sheetFor: Owners' representatives, asset managers, brand, insurance and compliance reviewers
⬇ PDF to print⬇ Word⬇ Markdown

What you get

A read-only Auditor account. You see every department’s jobs, rooms, food safety logs, incidents, reports, the audit log and the security page. The buttons are greyed out, so you cannot change anything by accident.

How to get an account

  1. Ask the property’s general manager or administrator for an Auditor account.
  2. They create it in Users with the role Auditor (read-only) and give you a temporary password in person or by a safe channel.
  3. Sign in, pick your own password and turn on two-step sign-in in My account. When the review ends, they untick Active.

What evidence exists

You want Where to look
Maintenance history Each asset’s jobs, labour and parts
PM done Ticked PM checklists. Reports, PM on time
Food safety (HACCP) Food safety: every reading, who, when, and the action taken
Inspections Completed checklists, scores, jobs created
Incidents Incident reports and follow-up jobs
Key control Key control: who had each master key, out and back
Guest service Reports: response, ratings, recovery
Vendor paperwork Vendors with insurance and WCB expiry dates
Security controls Security and compliance (NIST map)

Can I trust the record?

  • The Audit log records every sign-in and every change. Each line is chained to the one before, so a change or deletion breaks the chain.
  • Tap Verify integrity. A ✓ means the chain is intact.
  • People are never deleted, so their history stays.

Honest limits

  • The record is only as complete as the property’s use of ExpTix. Paper logs kept outside it are not here.
  • Without a PMS link, room status is typed by staff.
  • The audit log is kept for 7 years by default. The property sets this.
  • ExpTix is self-hosted. Ask who hosts it, how it is backed up, and where the data lives.
